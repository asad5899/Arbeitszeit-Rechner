Night shifts come with their own math puzzle. Beyond just calculating hours worked, you've got Nachtarbeitszuschlag rates, mandatory rest periods, and a shift that spans two separate calendar days. Getting all three right matters for both your paycheck and your health. This guide breaks down exactly how Nachtschicht berechnen works from start to finish.

## Wie wird eine Nachtschicht über zwei Kalendertage berechnen?
Treat the shift as one continuous block, regardless of the midnight crossing. A shift from 22:00 to 06:00 spans zwei Kalendertage but represents a single eight-hour work period.

Add 24 hours to your end time for the math to work cleanly. Thirty minus twenty-two equals eight hours total, exactly matching the real-world shift length.

## Wie unterscheidet man Arbeitszeit und Nachtzeit?
Arbeitszeit simply means hours actively worked. Nachtzeit refers to a specific legal window, often defined as 23:00 to 06:00, that triggers extra pay considerations regardless of your total shift length.

Not every night-shift hour necessarily falls within the legal night window. Understanding where that window starts and ends determines which hours qualify for additional Nachtzuschlag compensation.

## Wie werden Pausen innerhalb einer Nachtschicht berücksichtigt?
Break requirements don't relax just because the sun's down. An eight-hour night shift still needs the same 30-minute minimum break as any equivalent daytime shift under German Arbeitszeitgesetz rules.

Subtract breaks from gross shift time exactly as you would during the day. Pausen Nachtschicht calculations follow identical logic; only the clock hours look different.

## Wann können Nachtarbeitszuschläge relevant werden?
Many collective agreements and individual contracts include extra pay for hours worked during defined night windows. These Nachtzuschlag amounts vary significantly between industries and employers.

| Zeitfenster | Beispielzuschlag |
| :--- | :--- |
| 20:00–22:00 | Meist kein Zuschlag |
| 22:00–06:00 | Häufig 25% Zuschlag |
| Sonn- und Feiertagsnacht | Oft höherer Zuschlag |

Always check your specific contract or Tarifvertrag for exact percentages.

## Wie werden Schichten von 22:00 bis 06:00 Uhr berechnet?
This is the classic Nachtschicht 22 bis 6 pattern. Eight total hours, typically split with one 30-minute break, netting out to 7.5 hours of actual work time.

If the entire shift falls within a contractually defined night-pay window, all 7.5 net hours could qualify for the applicable Schichtzulage, depending on the specific agreement in place.

## Welche Ruhezeiten müssen zwischen zwei Schichten berücksichtigt werden?
German law requires a minimum 11-hour Ruhezeit between the end of one shift and the start of the next, regardless of whether the shift happened during the day or night.

For a shift ending at 06:00, the next shift generally cannot legally begin before 17:00 that same day, protecting workers from dangerously compressed rest windows.

## Wie berechnet man Nachtarbeit bei wechselnden Schichtzeiten?
Rotating schedules complicate things slightly. One week might run 22:00 to 06:00, while the next shifts to 20:00 to 04:00, changing exactly how much falls inside the legal night window.

Track each rotation separately rather than assuming a fixed pattern. Nachtarbeit berechnen accurately requires checking the actual start and end times for every single scheduled block.

## Wie werden Arbeitszeitkonto und Zuschlagszeiten getrennt dokumentiert?
Keep regular hours and night-differential hours in separate columns within your Arbeitszeitkonto. Mixing them together makes it nearly impossible to verify correct Nachtzuschlag payments later.

Clear separation also helps during payroll audits. A transparent breakdown between base pay hours and premium night hours avoids disputes over what's owed each month.

## Welche gesetzlichen und vertraglichen Regeln können zusätzlich gelten?
Beyond the general Arbeitszeitgesetz, specific industries and unions often negotiate additional protections through Betriebsvereinbarung agreements, covering things like maximum consecutive night shifts allowed.

Always review your own employment contract and any applicable collective agreement, since night-work rules vary considerably depending on sector, region, and specific employer arrangements.

## Fazit: Nachtschichten über Mitternacht korrekt erfassen
Calculating Nachtschicht hours correctly means treating the midnight crossing as a simple math adjustment, not an obstacle. Subtract breaks properly, identify which hours fall into premium Nachtzuschlag windows, and always respect mandatory rest periods between shifts for both compliance and your own wellbeing.
