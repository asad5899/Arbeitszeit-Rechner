Wie berechnet man die tägliche Arbeitszeit mit einer Pause?
Start with your total time on the clock, from Arbeitsbeginn to Arbeitsende. That's your raw attendance window, not yet your real working time.
Next, subtract your break length. If you arrived at 8:00 and left at 17:00, that's nine hours total. Take away a 30-minute lunch, and your Nettoarbeitszeit lands at 8.5 hours.
Wie wird die Pausenlänge vom Gesamtzeitraum abgezogen?
Subtracting Pausenzeit is basic arithmetic, but people mess it up constantly by forgetting to convert minutes properly. Thirty minutes equals half an hour, not "0.3" of an hour.
A clean formula helps: End time minus Start time minus Break time equals actual worked hours. Writing it this way avoids the mental gymnastics that often causes small, annoying errors.
Wie berechnet man Nettoarbeitszeit und Bruttoanwesenheit?
Bruttoarbeitszeit is everything, including breaks. Nettoarbeitszeit is what's left after breaks disappear. Payroll almost always cares about the net figure, not the gross one.
Confusing the two causes real problems. An employee reporting nine "worked" hours when only 8.5 were actually net time creates a mismatch that HR eventually has to untangle.

Long shifts often include more than one break. A 15-minute morning pause plus a 30-minute lunch adds up to 45 minutes total, all subtracted from your gross time.

Wie funktioniert die Berechnung bei einer festen Arbeitszeit?
Fixed schedules make things easier. If your contract says 8:00 to 16:30 with a fixed 30-minute lunch, your Arbeitszeit mit Mittagspause stays predictable every single day.
This predictability helps with monthly planning too. Multiply your daily net hours by working days in the month, and you'll get an accurate Arbeitszeitkonto estimate without surprises.
Wie berechnet man Arbeitszeit mit unterschiedlich langen Pausen?
Not every day looks the same. Maybe Monday's lunch ran 45 minutes because of a doctor's appointment, while Tuesday's stayed at the usual 30.
Track each day's actual break length separately rather than assuming a fixed number. Averaging breaks across a week only works if you're tracking real, not estimated, Pausenlänge every day.
Wie lassen sich Pausen bei Schichtarbeit berücksichtigen?
Shift workers often deal with irregular break placement. A night shift might include a break at 2 a.m. instead of noon, but the subtraction logic stays identical.
Whatever the shift pattern, always separate Arbeitszeit from break time in your notes. Lump-sum guessing at the end of a shift almost always produces a slightly wrong total.
